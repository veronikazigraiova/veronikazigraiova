Hello, I’m Vero.

I study what happens when people work with robots.

I’m a Doctoral Researcher in Human–Technology Interaction at Tampere University. My background brings together psychology, HCI and robotics.

My research examines how people understand unfamiliar automation, adapt their behaviour and decide when to trust it. I use interviews, questionnaires, Unity simulations and studies with physical robots.

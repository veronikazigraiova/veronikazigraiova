01 / About

People first.
Robots second.

I am a Doctoral Researcher in Human–Technology Interaction at Tampere University. My work sits between psychology, HCI and robotics.

My doctoral research focuses on trust in human–robot teaming, particularly in mixed-fleet logistics where people and different robotic systems share the same work.

I use quantitative, qualitative and experimental methods — from interviews and questionnaires to Unity simulations and physical robot studies.

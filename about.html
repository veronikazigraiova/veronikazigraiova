Background & interests

About me

Psychology, human–technology interaction and life around robots.

I am a Doctoral Researcher in Human–Technology Interaction at Tampere University. My background sits between psychology, HCI and robotics, which means I am usually less interested in whether a robot can do something than in what happens when a person has to work alongside it.

My doctoral research focuses on trust in human–robot teaming, particularly in mixed-fleet logistics environments where people, automated guided vehicles, mobile robots and other technologies share the same work.

I use quantitative, qualitative and experimental methods — from interviews and questionnaires to Unity simulations and physical robot studies.

My research

My PhD asks how trust develops and changes through interaction, and how robot behaviour, communication and training can support appropriate trust and safe collaboration.

Through the Mixed Fleet project, I study people and automation working together in logistics. My research visit to Bristol Robotics Laboratory explored collaboration involving an exoskeleton and a mobile robot.

Background & interests

About me

Hello there!

I’m a Doctoral Researcher in Human-Technology Interaction at Tampere University, Finland. My background brings together psychology, HCI and robotics. I’m curious about what happens when people encounter unfamiliar technology: how they make sense of it, when they trust it and what they do when its behaviour is unclear.

My doctoral research explores trust and collaboration between people, robots and automation, particularly in logistics workplaces. As part of the Mixed Fleet project at Robostudio, I study how people and automated vehicles share space and coordinate their work. I also spent two months at Bristol Robotics Laboratory, UWE Bristol, investigating how wearing an exoskeleton affects working with a mobile robot.

I’m originally from the Czech Republic and now live in Finland, having also lived in Wales and Spain. Outside research, I enjoy drawing, horror, metal and rock music and swimming. My creative projects include sculptural objects and a gothic puzzle game in Unity.

Research

I study how trust develops and changes through experience, and how robot behaviour, communication and training help people decide when to rely on automation. My work combines interviews, observation and questionnaires with experiments in Unity simulations and studies involving physical robots.

Design and development

I also work on interface design and interactive prototypes, drawing on my background in psychology to understand how people use technology. My projects include mobile and desktop prototypes in Figma, as well as Unity environments for studying interactions with automated vehicles.

Art and creative work

I draw, paint and make sculptural objects, often exploring gothic and horror themes. I’m also developing a gothic puzzle game in Unity, bringing together visual design, atmosphere and interaction. You’ll find these projects alongside my research and design work in my portfolio.

The concept

The Séance is a gothic puzzle game in development, designed for a vertical mobile screen.

Interaction and atmosphere

The opening ritual centres on an interactive candle puzzle. Atmospheric artwork, animated titles and a Victorian-inspired visual style establish the setting.

Development

The project is being developed in Unity and combines 2D interaction, game design and digital art. The image above shows the current menu in the Unity editor.

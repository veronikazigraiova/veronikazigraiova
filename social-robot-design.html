Portfolio / MSc research / Co-design

Designing interaction with social robots

My MSc research explored social robot interaction through co-design involving NAO and Elias.

Co-designSocial roboticsHuman–technology interaction

The context

This project formed part of my MSc research in Human–Technology Interaction at Tampere University.

The approach

The research explored social robot interaction through co-design involving NAO and Elias. Participatory design provided a way to bring people’s perspectives into the development and evaluation of interactive technology.

The focus

The work connects my background in psychology with social robotics, examining interaction through both human experience and design.

At a glance

Research project

The work brought psychological understanding together with participatory design and evaluation of interactive technology.

Profile

Researcher with a background in psychology and human–technology interaction, studying trust, communication, workload and coordination in human–robot collaboration. My work combines interviews, co-design, surveys, simulation and practical experiments with automated guided vehicles, mobile robots and wearable robotics.

Research interests: human–robot interaction; trust in automation; human–robot teaming; mixed-fleet logistics; multimodal interaction; cognitive workload; simulation-based training; industrial and wearable robotics.

Portfolio / Research / Multimodal interaction

Making robot activity easier to understand

Exploring haptic feedback, scent cues and a visual map as ways to support awareness in a simulated mixed-fleet warehouse.

Interaction designMultimodal feedbackUser evaluation

The question

What kinds of feedback help people understand nearby automation while they are occupied with their own task?

The approach

The study explores three forms of support within a simulated warehouse: haptic sleeves, scent cues delivered through an Aroma Shooter, and a visual map.

The evaluation

The research considers which signals participants notice and find useful when coordinating their movement with automated vehicles. The different modalities provide ways to investigate awareness beyond the vehicle’s visible behaviour.

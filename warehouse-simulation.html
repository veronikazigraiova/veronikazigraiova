Portfolio / Research / Unity simulation

Sharing a warehouse with robots

A Unity-based warehouse simulation exploring how people navigate shared spaces with automated guided vehicles. Scenarios include narrow corridors, intersections and deadlocks.

UnityExperimental researchHuman–robot interaction

The question

How do people navigate shared spaces when an automated vehicle’s behaviour is not yet familiar? This project examines both trust and the practical adjustments people make around automation.

The approach

The Unity warehouse simulation presents narrow corridors, intersections and deadlocks. These scenarios provide a setting for examining encounters between people and automated guided vehicles.

The research focus

The study considers how direct experience changes trust and how people adapt their behaviour around automated vehicles. It forms part of my doctoral research on mixed-fleet logistics.

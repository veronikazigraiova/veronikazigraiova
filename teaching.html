Teaching

Teaching Approach

I believe students learn best when they have opportunities to experiment, question assumptions and apply what they learn rather than simply memorising information.

I particularly enjoy creating practical and creative learning activities. With generative AI becoming increasingly common in education, I think it is important to help students understand how to use these tools critically without losing their own reasoning, judgement and creativity.

I also place considerable value on accessible feedback and individual support. My experience teaching large student cohorts motivated me to introduce additional HelpDesk sessions, giving students a space to ask questions and work through difficulties before submitting their assignments.
